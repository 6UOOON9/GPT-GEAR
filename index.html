Read the abstract +

We systematically evaluate GPT-6 Astra for closed-loop robotic manipulation through a lightweight agent execution framework. We probe depth perception, embodied in-context learning, and tactile understanding, then evaluate five manipulation benchmarks across six capability dimensions. The results reveal a gap between semantic reasoning and physical execution, especially in consequence-aware planning, precision, and complex motion coordination. We introduce LIBERO-GeoMotion to test geometry-constrained manipulation and validate the findings on a real robot. Contextual demonstrations improve execution, but motion feasibility, precise control, physical safety, and inference latency remain important limitations.
